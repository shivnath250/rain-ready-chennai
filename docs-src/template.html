Data, method and files

Rain levels follow IMD daily rainfall classes: dry or light (below 15.6 mm), moderate (15.6 to 64.4 mm), heavy (64.5 to 115.5 mm) and very heavy or extreme (115.6 mm and above). The rainfall series is synthetic (5 seasons of 92 days). Road costs, capacities and flood probabilities are illustrative values chosen for teaching. All of them can be replaced in the Excel and Python templates.

Section 2 runs a successive-shortest-path minimum-cost flow algorithm in JavaScript, which gives the same optimum as the transshipment LP in the Python and Excel files. Sections 1 and 4 are also computed live. Section 3 shows results from the Python stochastic LP.
